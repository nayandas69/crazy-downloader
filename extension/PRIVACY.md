Privacy Policy – YouTube Cookies Exporter

Last updated: November 3, 2026

## 1. Overview

YouTube Cookies Exporter is a browser extension developed as part of the Crazy Downloader project.

Its sole purpose is to allow users to export their own YouTube cookies into a Netscape-compatible cookies.txt file.

## 2. Information Accessed

When the user initiates an export, the extension accesses cookies associated with YouTube domains, including authentication and HttpOnly session cookies.

## 3. Local Processing

Cookies are processed locally in the browser and exported as a cookies.txt file. The extension does not independently upload cookies, browsing history, or personal information to its developer or third-party servers.

## 4. Optional Use With Crazy Downloader

Users may voluntarily provide their exported cookies to Crazy Downloader.

When they do, the web application stores the cookie text in browser localStorage and sends it to the Crazy Downloader backend in requests to YouTube. The backend uses the cookies for the requested operation and deletes its temporary cookie file after processing.

## 5. Analytics and Tracking

The extension does not include advertising, analytics, or browsing activity tracking.

## 6. User Control

Users decide whether to export cookies and where to save or use the exported file. Users should protect exported cookie files because they may contain active authentication sessions.
